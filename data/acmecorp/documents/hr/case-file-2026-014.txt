1. Subject

Employee number E-2291, Senior Specialist, Operations, Leipzig site. The employee's name is held in the HR case system and is left out of this file on purpose.

2. Type of case

Performance improvement plan, opened at the request of the employee's manager.

3. Background

The manager raised concerns in one-to-one meetings on 12 May and 23 June 2026. Both meetings are documented. The concerns were:

- shift handover reports filed late or incomplete on 9 occasions between April and June;
- two Level 2 warehouse incidents on the employee's shifts where the incident report was filed after the 24-hour limit;
- repeated complaints from the night shift about missing handover information.

HR confirmed that the conditions for opening a plan were met.

4. The plan

Start: 10 August 2026. Length: 60 days. End: 9 October 2026.

Objectives:

1. Every shift handover report filed before the end of the shift.
2. Every incident report filed within 24 hours.
3. No more than one handover complaint in any 4-week period.
4. Complete the supervisor reporting course by 15 September.

Support: weekly meeting with the manager, and a mentor from the Rotterdam site.

5. Notes

10 August: plan presented. The employee attended with a colleague and added a written comment that staffing on the late shift had been below plan since March.

8 September: midpoint review with the HR business partner. Objectives 1 and 4 met. One late incident report on 27 August. Two handover complaints in August. HR asked Operations to confirm the staffing figures the employee raised.

22 September: Operations confirmed the late shift was one person short on 31 of 60 shifts between March and June.

6. Current assessment

Clear progress, with objective 3 not yet met. Given the staffing finding, the case handler expects to recommend an extension of 30 days and not an outcome of "not passed".

7. Health information

The employee told HR on 8 September about a medical condition that affects night work. Details are held separately by occupational health and are not recorded here.

8. Next step

Final review meeting on 9 October 2026.
